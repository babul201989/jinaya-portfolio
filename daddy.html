A Letter from Daddy

A little note from the heart, for you to read now and again when you grow up.

To my dearest Jinu

My precious daughter,

From the moment you came into my life, you gave my world a whole new meaning. Your tiny hands, innocent eyes, little smiles and every sound you make have become some of my favourite things.

I know you are still too little to understand these words. One day, when you are older, I hope you come back to this page and see just how deeply you were loved from the very beginning.

I may not be a perfect father, but I promise to keep learning, keep showing up and keep trying to give you the love, care and confidence you deserve. I want to be there for your first steps, your big dreams, your difficult days and every little moment in between.

Never feel that you have to become someone else to make me proud. Be kind, be curious, be brave, and always be yourself. No matter how big you grow or where life takes you, you will always be my little Jinu.

This website is just a small collection of memories. My real treasure is you.

With all my love,
Daddy ❤️
